STEP ONE

Two minutes, either way. Sign in with the same Google or Apple account you will keep using.

YOUR PHONE

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store. It is Apple's own app for beta versions.
  2. Come back here and tap Join the beta.
  3. Open The Forge from TestFlight and sign in.
Join the beta on iPhone

YOUR PHONE

Android

through Google Play
  1. Tap Join the beta below and accept on the page that opens.
  2. Use the Google account that is signed in to your Play Store.

STEP THREE

Week one.
In this order.

Each one tests a different part of the system. The last one is the one nobody does unless asked.

  1. Write your Creed and set one commitment.Who you are becoming, and the first thing you will prove it with.
  2. Run one real focus session.Mid session, try to open an app you blocked. Watch what happens.
  3. Let Dark Hours reach you.When the app locks for the night, write the reflection, then sleep.
  4. Bring two brothers into a Triad.Three people who see every win and every miss.
  5. Break a commitment on purpose.Once. See what it does to you. That feeling is the product.
