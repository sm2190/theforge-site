Support and Billing

Last updated 3 November 2026

Everything about your membership, payments and account in one place. If something here does not answer it, email us and a real person replies.

Manage or cancel your membership

  1. Open the billing portal.
  2. Enter the email you paid with. Stripe sends you a sign-in link.
  3. Update your card, download invoices, or cancel. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you already paid for.

Didn't get your verification code?

The 6-digit code goes to the email you type into the app, so type the email you paid with. Check Spam and Promotions. A code works for 20 minutes, so request a new one in the app if it is old. Still nothing after 10 minutes? Email us from the address you paid with.
